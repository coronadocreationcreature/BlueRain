Learning outcome 02 · Ethics & society

Ethics, society, and responsible AI.

Identify and discuss ethical issues associated with AI and ML and their societal implications.

My reflection

There are certain ethical issues associated with AI and ML and their societal implications. These issues include algorithmic bias, which can unintentionally target minorities or discriminate based on past data unfairly. Another of these issues is dual use, which is when an AI system is built for good and has practical use but is misused and can be manipulated for malpractice as not intended by the designer of the system. Another ethical issue associated with AI systems is dual use which is when AI systems that are designed for good can be misused for ill intent. Many AI systems are intended for good by the designer however the burden of ethical issues falls on both the creator and user when it comes to dual use.

Many people would blame only AI however it is a tool just like any other thing that can be weaponized. Evil people with malicious intent will use AI and other tools, even if they are designed for good, to do bad things and abuse the power of these tools. It is especially important to consider this when talking about AI. AI has a bad reputation for its dual use; cheating in class, inappropriate generating, inaccurate prescribing, nonconsensual abuse, etc. However this isn’t fully an AI problem. As every other tool does, AI also has downsides. The burden of guilt of these downsides however should heavily fall on the abuser of the tool and not the tool itself. The design being abused and misused isn’t the fault of the AI but of the user.

Regulations should be put on AI especially for large scale government uses of it but to ban or restrict it for everyone would be unfair. Dual use when it comes to AI systems’ ethics is when a system is deliberately exploited in a way the designer of the system didn’t anticipate. This hurts both the clients who could lose access to the system if it is shut down for security reasons but also those who could be harmed by the misuse of the AI. This affects the community for which it was inherently designed to help as they can lose access to the system and its benefits. The dual use falls ethically more on the people misusing the AI tools and systems as they are exploiting it not as intended to hurt a group. The AI systems are designed with good intentions but it is up to the client and those who can access the systems ethically to not take advantage of them or misuse them for malice.
