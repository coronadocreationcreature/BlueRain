How I found my path

A crossword puzzle changed the picture.

I’ve always been interested in engineering, especially the software side. Last winter, when I visited Drexel as an admitted student in general engineering, I spoke with Dr. Dario Salvucci about the new AI major. His enthusiasm drew me in. He showed us an AI simulator modeling code for every possible outcome of a crossword puzzle. Seeing that in action was mind-blowing—and one of the first times AI really clicked for me. I’m a four-year, one-co-op student and am considering graduating a year early.

What motivates me

Learning through real challenges

I love learning and applying what I know to problems that matter. Math and computer science feel like puzzles: I enjoy the adversity before the solution, and a class without that challenge can feel unsatisfying.

MY FAVORITE LEARNING OUTCOME

Search, strategy, and intelligent behavior

I’m most interested in how core AI techniques like search and adversarial reasoning model intelligent behavior. I find it fascinating to analyze how AI models use mathematics to search through possibilities and build models of their world.
