Learning outcome 01 · Historical foundations

AI and ML: from their history to today.

Explain key moments in the historical development of AI and ML and relate them to modern AI systems.

My reflection

Many people think of Artificial Intelligence as a new, up and coming technology that hasn’t existed before ChatGPTs giant boom in 2022 however this is not true and AI stories go back to the 8th century and machinery dates back to even the 18th century. AI goes back to the beginnings of time starting with stories of things like the golem and talos which included myth and folklore stories about automation and animated creatures and machines. Computing artificial intelligence started with Alan Turing in 1950 with the Turing test. Many people modernly only think that current computers and generative AI are the start and history of AI however AI is simply machine learning and automated machinery. The Turing test was a game proposed to decide if machines can think and even beat humans in games. Alan Turing had humans judge transcripts to try and guess which was written by humans and which by artificial intelligence. The machine wins if the person can’t deliberate between the humans and machines way of speaking. Turing published a paper on this called “Computing Machinery and Intelligence” which begs the question that has led to AI philosophical debates that last today, “Can machines think?”

It doesn’t stop there however, AI, as many know, has continued to boom since. The term “artificial intelligence” itself was coined by Dartmouth summer research students in 1956 and this is when the field of AI itself was birthed. In 1956 and 1957 more advancements were made including AI using logical mathematical rules to reason problems and general problem solving using machine learning was developed. Similar to the current ChatGPT models that most people tend to think of when they hear AI, the first AI chatbot named Eliza was created in 1966 using pattern matching and this began the stemming development of other different types of AI such as generative and descriptive AI as we know today.

Artificial intelligence continued to struggle though with real world complexity and failed to scale to realities messiness. This was known as the first AI winter and was during the 70s, this lasted until AI went commercial in the 80s. Fortune 500 companies (up to 58% of them) started using AI for their businesses and many digital equipment were created including MYCIN (prescriptive AI to organize medicine prescriptions) and XCON (configured computer orders, another prescriptive AI algorithm). These new algorithms and machines weren’t perfect though and a second AI winter came in the 90s. This was short lived though as in 1997 researchers of AI switched to more statistical methods and this was a success.

Around the same time, the internet and GPUs became prevalent and this boosted the use of AI even more. AI became a deeper learning network rather than a plug and punch machine starting with AlexNet. AlexNet was a deep neural network that correctly identified objects in over a million photos and blew the runner up out of the water. Even though this neural network was successful, it originated in 1957 and took 70 years before finally properly used. This was when people started to think of the AI neuron as a normal neuron and translated previous knowledge to describe the digital evolution of AI. In 2017, the boost of AI continued as researchers introduced a larger scale design that was quicker and much more efficient which effectively set the stage for ChatGPTs worldwide impact in 2022. Now AI is still important and growing with many different types of algorithms such as generative, descriptive, prescriptive, etc.
